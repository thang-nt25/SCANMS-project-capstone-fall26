# Live reaction assets

3D PNG icons from [Microsoft Fluent Emoji](https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji), distributed under the MIT license in LICENSE.txt.
Names: Red heart, Thumbs up (Default), Face with tears of joy, Face with open mouth, Crying face, Pouting face, Fire, Wrapped gift, Party popper, Hundred points, Glowing star.
Floating hearts also use Pink heart, Orange heart, Purple heart, Light blue heart and Yellow heart from the same collection.
Bundled locally so reactions do not depend on third-party image hosts.

## Animated reactions

`animated/*.webp` and their `animated/*.png` still frames are Animated Noto Emoji
by Google, licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Official catalog: https://googlefonts.github.io/noto-emoji-files/?view=docs
Each original Google asset URL and downloaded/optimised file hash is listed in `animated/manifest.json`.
Changes: resized to 128 px, WebP re-encoded for the Live UI; PNG still frames extracted
for reduced-motion preferences. See `animated/LICENSE-CC-BY-4.0.txt`.
These are independent open assets, not TikTok or Shopee's proprietary icon pack.
